# The Serial Guest Blogger Prospecting Method V2

**Objective:** To generate a hyper-targeted list of high-authority guest posting opportunities by identifying and reverse-engineering the success of prolific, respected authors in a client's niche. This method is designed to uncover pre-vetted publications that are receptive to expert, data-driven content.

---

## Phase 1: Identification of "Serial Guest Bloggers"

* **Step 1.1: Identify Potential High-Value Authors**  
  * **Objective:** To create a seed list of 5-10 influential and prolific authors in your client's specific industry.  
  * **Procedure:**  
    * **Analyze Competitor Backlinks:** Use a tool like Ahrefs to analyze the backlink profiles of 2-3 of your client's top competitors. Look for authors who have written guest posts for them on high-DR websites.  
    * **Use `Exa Websets`:** Run a query to find authors who are frequently published on top-tier industry blogs.

      **`Exa` Prompt:**  
      "Generate a dataset of all articles published in the last 12 months on `[Top Industry Blog A]`, `[Top Industry Blog B]`, and `[Top Industry Blog C]`. For each article, include the **Author's Full Name** and the **Author's Bio**. Identify and list the authors who have appeared most frequently across these publications."

    * **Google Search:** Use advanced search queries like `"guest post by" [Industry Expert Name]` or `"[Industry Keyword]" + "author"` to find individuals known for their writing.  
  * **Output:** A list of 5-10 potential "serial guest bloggers."

* **Step 1.2: Qualify the Authors**  
  * **Objective:** To ensure the authors on your list are credible and have a portfolio worth analyzing.  
  * **Procedure:** For each author, perform a quick check:  
    * **\[ \] Credibility:** Does this person have a strong professional background (e.g., a well-maintained LinkedIn profile, a personal website)?  
    * **\[ \] Prolificacy:** A quick search of their name reveals guest posts on multiple different websites.  
    * **\[ \] Quality:** The sites they have written for are reputable and meet your agency's general quality standards.

---

## Phase 2: Reverse-Engineering Placements

* **Step 2.1: Execute Reverse Image Search Prospecting**  
  * **Objective:** To rapidly and efficiently build a list of all the publications where your target author has been published.  
  * **Procedure:**  
    1. **Find the Author's Headshot:** Go to your target author's LinkedIn profile, personal blog, or a recent guest post and save a high-quality version of their headshot.  
    2. **Use Google Reverse Image Search:** Go to Google Images and upload the author's headshot.  
    3. **Analyze the Results:** The search results will show a list of websites where that specific image appears. The vast majority of these will be author bios from their guest posts.  
    4. **Compile the Prospect List:** For each unique and relevant publication that appears in the search results, add the **Website Name** and the specific **URL of the guest post** to your prospecting spreadsheet.  
  * **Output:** A raw list of 10-50+ potential guest post opportunities per author.

* **Step 2.2: Deepen the Search with `Exa Websets`**  
  * **Objective:** To find placements that the reverse image search might have missed (e.g., if the author used a different headshot).  
  * **Procedure:** Run a targeted `Exa Websets` prompt for your top 2-3 qualified authors.  
  * **`Exa` Prompt:**  
    **"Generate a dataset of all articles published in the last 3 years where `[Author's Full Name]` is listed as the author, excluding articles from their own company's website.** **For each entry, include the URL and the Publication Name."**  
  * **Action:** Add any new, unique publications found to your prospecting spreadsheet.

---

## Phase 3: Final Vetting and Integration

* **Step 3.1: Execute Rigorous Prospect Vetting**  
  * **Objective:** To ensure every publication on your newly generated list meets the strict quality criteria of your main Guest Posting SOP.  
  * **Procedure:** For each publication on your list, perform the full vetting process:  
    * **\[ \] Audience Relevance:** Is their audience a perfect match?  
    * **\[ \] Content Quality:** Is the content professional and well-edited?  
    * **\[ \] Authority (DR):** Does it meet your minimum Domain Rating standard?  
    * **\[ \] Engagement:** Do their articles have an active readership?  
    * **\[ \] No "Pay-to-Play":** Does the site seem to be a genuine editorial publication?

* **Step 3.2: Integrate into the Main SOP**  
  * **Objective:** To feed your final, high-quality prospect list back into your primary workflow.  
  * **Procedure:** The final, vetted list of publications from this process now becomes the input for **Phase 2: Personalization Research & Angle Development** in your "Definitive Guide to Guest Posting for Authority." You can now proceed with identifying the correct editor and conducting the deep personalization research required to craft your pitch.
