Definitive Guide to Linkable Asset Outreach V0
Objective To systematically acquire high-quality, editorially-placed backlinks by promoting your high-value Source Magnets (Type A, B, or C) as essential resources for content creators, editors, and journalists. This strategy is built on providing targeted value, not making requests.

Phase 1 Asset Deconstruction & Prospecting
Step 1.1 Deconstruct Your Source Magnet into Data Hooks
Objective To break down your finished Source Magnet into its most citable, high-impact components.
Procedure
Review your Source Magnet and identify the 3-5 most surprising, compelling, or commercially relevant headline stats or core findings.
For each finding, create a concise, one-sentence Data Hook. This is the specific piece of value you will offer in your outreach.
Enter each Data Hook into your outreach tracking spreadsheet.
Step 1.2 Hunt for Data Deficits with exa.ai
Objective For each Data Hook, to generate a hyper-targeted list of prospects whose existing content would be significantly improved by including your specific statistic.
Procedure
For each Data Hook, formulate a targeted exa.ai prompt designed to find content with a data deficit.
Template The Data Deficit exa.ai Prompt
Generate a dataset of all articles and blog posts published in the last 18 months that discuss [The general topic of your Data Hook] but DO NOT contain a specific percentage, dollar amount, or the phrase 'a study by'.

For each URL in the provided list, find and include the following fields”
The Full Name of the article's author
The Direct Verified Email Address of author.
The Job Title of the author.
A link to the author's professional LinkedIn or XTwitter profile.
The Title and URL of the most recent article published by that author on the same website.
The full text of the author's most recent post or tweet from their professional social media profile.


Execute this prompt for each of your Data Hooks. The output is your initial list of high-potential prospect URLs.

Phase 2 Vetting & Personalization Research
Step 2.1 Execute Rigorous Prospect Vetting
Objective To filter your initial prospect list down to only high-quality, relevant, and authoritative websites.
Procedure Follow your established SOP for vetting. Each prospect URL must meet the following criteria
Relevance The website's overall topic is a strong fit for your client's industry.
Content Quality The existing content is well-written, professional, and credible.
Authority The website has a minimum Domain Rating (DR) of [Your Agency's Standard, e.g., 50+].
Traffic The website has legitimate, consistent organic traffic.
Step 2.2 Conduct Deep Personalization Research
Objective To gather the specific, non-generic details needed to craft a pitch that demonstrates genuine interest and research.
Procedure For each prospect that passes the vetting stage, you must complete the following checklist. This is a non-negotiable step.
Template The Personalization Checklist
Refer to step 1.2
Manual Reference (Optional Fallback)
For extremely high-value targets or if the automated process fails to retrieve a key piece of information, you can refer back to the manual checklist. This ensures quality control and allows for deeper, more nuanced research when warranted.
Manual Checklist Reference
Find the Right Person [Content Manager, Editor, or Author]
Find Their Full Name & Title
Find Their Direct Email (using contact-finding tools)
Identify the Target URL
Find Their Most Recent Article
Research Their Recent Social Media Activity

Phase 3 Crafting the Personalized Pitch
Step 3.1 Select the Unique Pitching Angle
Objective To choose the correct strategic angle based on the type of Source Magnet you are promoting.
Procedure Refer to our Definitive Guide to Promotional Pitching Angles and select the appropriate angle
For a Type A Magnet The Data-Backed Business Case
For a Type B Magnet The Definitive, Citable Benchmark
For a Type C Magnet The Definitive Explanatory Model
Step 3.2 Write the Personalized Pitch
Objective To craft a unique, compelling, and value-driven email that uses your personalization research to make an irresistible offer.
Procedure Fill in the following master template.
Template The Linkable Asset Outreach Pitch
Subject Line A question or a direct reference to their work. Examples A question about your article on [Topic], Data for your [Topic] article
Body
Hi [First Name],
I was reading through [Publication Name] and saw your excellent article, [Target URL Article Title].
[Insert Personalized Comment Here - Reference their recent article or social media post. Be genuine.] Example I especially appreciated your recent post on LinkedIn about the future of [Industry]. Your point about [X] was spot-on.
The reason I'm reaching out is that my team at [Your Client's Company Name] just published a comprehensive data study on this exact topic, [Source Magnet Title], and our findings might add a powerful new statistic to support your article.
Specifically, [Insert Your Unique Pitching Angle Here, tailored to the asset type]. For example, our data shows
